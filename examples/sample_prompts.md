# Sample Prompts

Try these against ChatGPT (custom GPT) or Grok once the bridge is running.

## Cursor themes

1. "Apply the apple_cursor high-contrast theme."
2. "What cursor themes do you have?"
3. "Switch to the large_pointer theme - I keep losing my cursor on calls."
4. "Use the remote_work_clear theme for my next screen share."

## Profiles

5. "Create a remote work profile and activate it."
6. "What profiles do I have available?"
7. "Apply the Accessibility Power User profile." (warns before enabling VoiceOver)
8. "Build a custom profile named 'Night Shift' with pointer 2.0 and max contrast."
9. "Switch to Developer Focus and tell me what changed."

## Direct settings

10. "Set my pointer size to 2.0 for accessibility."
11. "Bump display contrast to max - the glare in this cafe is brutal."
12. "Turn contrast back to normal."
13. "Enable VoiceOver for screen reading."
14. "Is VoiceOver running right now?"

## Status & backups

15. "Backup my current macOS settings."
16. "How is my Mac configured right now?"
17. "Restore the settings from my latest backup."
18. "Before you change anything, back up my settings - then apply high contrast."

## Accessibility advice

19. "My eyes hurt after long sessions - suggest some accessibility improvements."
20. "Explain what full keyboard navigation does and turn it on."
21. "I'm on a video call and people can't see my cursor - what should I do?"
